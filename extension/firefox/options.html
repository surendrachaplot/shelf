shelf

Browser extension

Saved links open in the shelf web app at this address.

How to save

Click the shelf button in the toolbar. It saves the page you are on.

Right-click a link, then choose Save to shelf. Text you selected goes with it.

Press Alt + Shift + S. On a Mac, Option + Shift + S.

Where it goes

Your shelf is kept in this browser. It does not sync with your phone yet.
