Is the £45 kettle any better than the £12 one?

We boiled the same litre of water forty times in each. The expensive one, at £45.00, was eleven seconds faster.

The cheap one costs £12 and has a lid that comes off in your hand. Over five years the difference in electricity is about $1,299 for nobody at all; it is pennies.

If you want one that will last, the one at £45 is the one we would buy. See it at the shop (£45).
