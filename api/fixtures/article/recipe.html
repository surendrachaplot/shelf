Brown butter lemon dal

My grandmother made dal every single day of her life and would have been appalled by the idea of putting brown butter anywhere near it. I am sorry, and also I am not.

This started as a mistake. I had butter on the stove for something else, forgot about it while the lentils boiled, and came back to a pan that smelled like hazelnuts and toffee.

It was too good to throw out and there was nothing else in the house, so it went into the pot. We have made it that way most weeks since, and nobody has asked for the old version back.

A few notes before you start. Toor dal is what I use, but red lentils work and cook in half the time. Do not skip the lemon; it is what stops the whole thing tasting like pudding.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp cumin seed

Method

  1. Boil the dal until soft.
  2. Brown the butter with the cumin.
  3. Stir together and add lemon.
