Fellside Wool Blanket, Charcoal

£145.00

Woven in a mill in the Borders that has been making blankets on the same looms since 1911, from the fleece of sheep that graze within sight of the building.

The wool is washed in soft river water and left undyed where it can be, which is why no two blankets are exactly the same shade and why this one is called charcoal and not black.

It is heavy in the way good blankets are. It will last longer than the sofa you put it on, and it gets softer every winter for about the first ten years.

We sell it with a twenty year repair promise. Send it back with a hole in it and the mill will darn it and send it home, and you pay for the postage and nothing else.
